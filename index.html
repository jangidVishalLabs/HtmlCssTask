    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can I take the same type of course more than once and receive CE credits?
    An example of this would be completing an additional 3 CE hours of Ethics resulting in a total of 6 hours of Ethics. Yes, you can. The additional 3 CE hours of Ethics would then go towards your General CE requirements. But you cannot take the same course (same NRED CE #) for a second time and receive the additional credits. Prior to submitting your completed CE certificates, verify that you do not have duplicate courses.
